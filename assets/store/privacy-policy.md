# Noir privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Noir ("the app") is a media player for LG webOS TVs. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: it does not collect your data, and nothing you enter in the app is sent to us.

## What the app stores on your TV

To work, the app keeps the following on your TV only:
- The server address, username and password you enter to sign in, and the other accounts you choose to save
- Your settings, favourites, watch progress, recently watched channels and a cached copy of the lists your provider returns, so the app opens quickly

This information never leaves your TV through the app. You can remove it by signing out in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

## Who the app talks to

The app connects only to the server you enter. It requests your channels, movies, series, artwork and video streams from that server, and may load artwork from the addresses your provider includes in its data. Those services see your TV's network address and the requests the app makes, as with any video player, and are governed by their own policies.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect, receive or sell personal data
- We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking
- We do not create accounts or ask for an email address
- The app has no way for us to see what you watch or which provider you use

## Content

Noir does not provide, host or sell any content. All channels, movies and series come from the provider you choose. You are responsible for using a provider and content you are entitled to.

## Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
