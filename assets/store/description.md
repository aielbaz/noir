**Description (50–4000 characters):**

Noir is a fast, minimal media player for your own Xtream Codes IPTV subscription. Sign in with the server address, username and password your provider gave you, and your live channels, movies and series appear in a clean, dark interface built for the remote.

Noir does not include, sell or provide any channels or content. You need your own subscription from a provider you are entitled to use.

What you get
• Home with a rotating banner of new titles, rows from the categories you choose, and Continue watching so you pick up where you left off
• Live TV, Movies and TV Shows, each with category browsing, search and sorting
• Resume playback for every movie and episode, plus a watched badge
• Audio track selection for videos with several languages
• My list for the titles you want to keep
• Autoplay next episode and an Up next card for series
• Built for the TV: smooth on very large lists, works with the arrow keys and OK, and with the Magic Remote pointer and wheel
• Several accounts can be saved and switched on the login screen
• Dark design that stays out of the way of what you are watching

Your login and settings stay on your TV. Noir has no accounts, ads or tracking.
