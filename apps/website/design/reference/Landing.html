Questions.

Does Noir include channels or movies?+

No. Noir is the app you watch with. The channels and movies come from your own TV subscription, which you sign in to.

What kind of subscription do I need?+

One that gives you an Xtream Codes login: a server address, a username and a password. Your provider can tell you if you’re not sure.

Which TVs does it work on?+

LG smart TVs running webOS [SUPPORTED VERSIONS].

Is Noir free?+

[PRICE]

Is my login safe?+

Your sign-in details are stored on your TV and used only to connect to the server you enter. There’s no Noir account and no Noir server. Signing out clears everything.

Will my videos play?+

Noir plays the common streaming and video formats (HLS, MP4 and MKV). Subtitles built into MKV files may not show, depending on the TV.

Does it work with the Magic Remote?+

Yes. You can point and click, and scroll with the wheel. The regular arrow buttons work everywhere too.
