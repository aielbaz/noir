Privacy

Last updated [DATE]

Noir has no account, no ads and no tracking. It doesn’t collect personal data about you.

What stays on your TV

To work, Noir saves your login, your settings, My list, what you’ve watched and your channel lists on your TV. None of it is sent to us. Noir has no server of its own.

Your provider

Noir connects straight to your TV subscription provider. Noir doesn’t supply any channels or movies. Your provider’s own terms and privacy policy cover what they show you and any data they handle.

What Noir doesn’t do

  • No tracking of what you do
  • No advertising
  • No sale or sharing of your data

Removing your data

Sign out in Settings to clear everything Noir has stored on your TV. Uninstalling the app removes it too.
